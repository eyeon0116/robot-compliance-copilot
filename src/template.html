EU MACHINERY REGULATION

The EU machinery rules are being rewritten

The safety law for selling machinery in the EU is being replaced. Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 takes over from the 2006 Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and applies directly in every member state from 20 January 2027. Service robots are covered.

WHY THE DATE MATTERS

The robot you are building now ships under the new rules

From that day, robots entering the EU need the new safety requirements, technical documentation and CE marking in place. New requirements cover autonomous mobility, self-learning safety functions and protection against hacking. Most robots in development today will launch after that date, so their safety documents should be written to the new rules now.
